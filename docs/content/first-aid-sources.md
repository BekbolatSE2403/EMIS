# First aid content scope and review

FR-12 remains Could scope. This revision specifies an editorial process; it adds no treatment instructions to the product and claims no clinical approval.

The prototype may link to general first-aid education. Any future locally hosted summary must name its exact source/version, intended audience, reviewer and review date, retain medical context and emergency-service direction, and distinguish education from personalized advice. No diagnosis, medication recommendation or patient-specific treatment is generated from profile fields.

Candidate source verified at the publisher on 2 November 2026: [IFRC International First Aid, Resuscitation and Education Guidelines 2025](https://www.ifrc.org/document/ifrc-international-first-aid-resuscitation-and-education-guidelines-2025). The publisher lists its release in March 2026. This establishes a candidate reference, not review or approval of any proposed EMIS medical text. Check source permissions and exact recommendations before reproducing material.

Shugyla coordinates editorial/source records; the team must obtain a qualified medical review before publishing instructions. Until that happens, provide general emergency-services guidance and clearly labeled links only. Recheck after source updates and remove outdated/unreviewed summaries. T-14 in verification.md defines the release gate.
